# Live-participant axis replay — 2026-08-26

## Scope

Ten previously recorded human free-response sessions were re-evaluated with the two-stage,
role-free axis evaluator. Four sessions contained follow-up question-answer exchanges. Those
transcripts were evaluated again at every cumulative prefix.

The multi-turn portion is a counterfactual re-evaluation of actual old transcripts. It does not
claim that the participant answered questions generated by the new evaluator.

## Contract finding

The first replay exposed one valid partial-axis state in which every atomic relation was confirmed
but the macro connection remained unclear. The old axis contract incorrectly required an UNCLEAR
target to be an `unknown` relation and rejected the result.

The role-free contract now allows a partial axis to cite confirmed relations that locate the missing
macro connection. Legacy relation-targeted prompts still require exactly one unknown relation.

## Over-question finding and gold refinement

The first successful replay showed that four axis descriptions were still detailed rewrites rather
than minimum sufficient pass meanings. They asked for a blood-brain-barrier label, an internal globus
pallidus step, the lower-level Hegelian synthesis, or detailed autoreceptor signaling even when the
student had already expressed the axis's central direction.

The core relations were preserved for representation and error recording. Only the pass-axis
descriptions were compressed:

- Dopamine p1: external versus brain-synthesized dopamine; synthesis and inter-neuron signaling.
- Dopamine p2: presynaptic D2 autoreceptor as dopamine self-regulation.
- Dopamine p3: opposite thalamic and motor outcomes of the direct and indirect pathways.
- Hegel p1: synthesis of art's objectivity and religion's subjectivity in philosophy.

The D1/D2 receptor-placement basis in dopamine p4 was retained. Its follow-up question is an intended
effect of the newly approved central story, not an accidental detail question.
