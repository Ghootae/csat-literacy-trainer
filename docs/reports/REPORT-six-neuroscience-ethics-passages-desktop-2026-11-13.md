## Science checks and limits

- [Libet et al. 1983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6640273/) investigated reported intention timing relative to readiness potential and movement; [Schurger et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1210467109) demonstrated an accumulator interpretation of the **average** premovement rise. This supports 가/나's caution. It does **not** prove the accumulator is the actual sole mechanism, nor date decision completion in each trial.
- [Mehta et al. 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10704519/) found greater working-memory benefit from methylphenidate among participants with lower baseline task capacity. It did not directly establish each participant's baseline dopamine level or precise position on an inverted-U curve. Thus 라 can present a possible boundary challenge, but 바's stronger chain from dopamine level to benefit overstates the experiment.
- [Healthy-adult methylphenidate single-dose review](https://academic.oup.com/ijnp/article/17/6/961/692761) supports domain/task heterogeneity and DA/NA transporter effects in 다. [Volkow et al. 1998 PET](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9766762/) measured human dopamine-transporter occupancy, and [Ilieva et al. 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25591060/) reported small inhibitory-control effects for prescription stimulants including amphetamine and methylphenidate, as distinguished in 마. None establishes long-term enhancement safety or actual examination performance.
- The philosophical verdicts here are internal consistency and editorial judgments. We did not test whether high-school readers comprehend each paragraph, nor whether the passages support unique keyed questions.

## Passage-specific repair targets

**가:** Retain measurement/interpretation separation. Explain that “강한 선결정론” is a position defined for this text; reduce repeated criteria and caveats. Quote to protect condition: “이후의 의도는 그 결정의 형성에 관여하지 못한다” holds only under the explicitly assumed completed-decision case.

**나:** Distinguish average RP onset from initiation of an individual action. Quote: “선행 준비 전위의 시작은 해당 행동의 실행을 정하는 과정의 시작이라고 가정해 보자.” This is a stipulated mapping, not an observed trial-level fact. Clarify whether “의식적 개시” concerns first neural antecedent or conscious causal contribution. Avoid generalizing the simple movement conclusion to morally significant actions.

**다:** Resolve ㉯'s apparent scope clash. Quote pair: “해당 경쟁 수행의 향상이 확인된 경우 … 때에만 공정성 측면에서 허용” / “경쟁상 이익이 없다고 확인되면 공정성을 이유로 제한하지 않지만”. Specify fairness verdict for confirmed benefit, confirmed no benefit, and unknown benefit. Reduce repeated named criteria if readability matters.

**라:** Keep the normal-function boundary case. Qualify “누구나 접근할 수 있게 하면 … 격차가 줄어들 수 있다” as a possibility/hypothesis; the cited baseline-benefit result does not prove population gap reduction. “약물이 공부를 덜 지루하게 만든다면” is already conditional; keep that status salient and do not imply that dopamine's general role proves a real studying outcome. Clarify that coverage policy is an added normative rule, not a logical entailment of the treatment/enhancement classification.

**마:** Most ready for item development. Define who sets “사전에 정한 허용 한도” and whether individual goal-task improvement needs repeated confirmation. Trim condition stacking. Source distinction in paragraph 2 is unusually careful and should be preserved.

**바:** Rebuild rather than line-edit. Quote: “평소 수준이 적정 수준보다 낮은 사람은 작업 기억 과제의 수행도 낮은 경향이 있다. 그래서 같은 약을 먹어도 … 이미 적정한 수준에 있는 사람은 효과가 거의 없거나 오히려 수행이 떨어질 수 있다는 연구 결과가 있다.” This compresses a plausible inverted-U model, baseline-performance moderation, and unmeasured individual dopamine position into a seemingly established causal rule. “분비된 뒤 … 다시 흡수되며, 이로써 신호가 끝난다” oversimplifies signal termination. “정상적인 인지 능력을 그 이상으로 끌어올리는” makes healthy low performers awkward to classify.
